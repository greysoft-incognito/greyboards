Full description (Play Store and App Store, 4000 characters or fewer)
Your brand, on billboards anywhere.

Greyboards puts real billboards in your pocket. Explore boards on a map, see exactly where they stand and who passes by, book the one you love and watch your ad go up, all without leaving your desk.

FIND THE RIGHT BOARD
Browse billboards, gantries, bus shelters, walls and digital screens on a live map. Every board shows its size, which way it faces, whether it's lit at night, roughly how many people pass it each day and what it costs, in your own currency.

BOOK IN MINUTES
Pick a start date, choose how long to run and upload your design. Printing and posting are handled by local pros, and you only pay once. Your money is held safely until your ad is up.

SHARE A BOARD
Want a big board for less? Share it with other brands for the same period and split the cost. Everyone's design is merged into one eye-catching print.

WATCH YOUR AD GO UP
Get photos of your ad on the board, taken on the spot, and see a report of your campaign when it ends. Digital screens show how many times your ad played.

PUT YOUR BOARD TO WORK
Own a billboard? List it, set your rates and get booked by advertisers you'd never reach on your own. Approve designs, follow every booking and get paid once the ad is up.

SPOT A BOARD, EARN FROM IT
Know a board that isn't listed yet? Tag it from where you stand, sign the owner up and earn a share of every booking it brings in.

PRINT, POST, GET PAID
Printers and posters can find jobs nearby, set their own prices and get paid once the photos are accepted.

PEOPLE YOU CAN TRUST
Every owner, scout and poster builds a reputation from real work. Badges like Verified and Proven show you who to count on.

Greyboards is starting in a few cities and growing fast. Wherever your audience is, we want your brand to be there too.
