# Store listing

Screenshots and copy for Google Play and the App Store. The screens are the real mobile app running on the `db:mock` demo data.

| Folder | Size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `play/feature-graphic.png` | 1024 × 500 | Play feature graphic |
| `play/phone/` | 1080 × 1920 | Play phone screenshots |
| `play/tablet-7/` | 1440 × 2560 | Play 7-inch tablet screenshots |
| `play/tablet-10/` | 1800 × 3200 | Play 10-inch tablet screenshots |
| `app-store/iphone-6.9/` | 1320 × 2868 | iPhone 6.9" display |
| `app-store/iphone-6.5/` | 1242 × 2688 | iPhone 6.5" display |
| `app-store/ipad-13/` | 2064 × 2752 | iPad 13" display |

Each set has the same eight screens, numbered in upload order:

1. Find: "Your brand, on billboards anywhere"
2. A board: "Know the board before you book"
3. Shared board: "Share a board, split the cost"
4. Proof of posting: "Watch your ad go up"
5. Owner home: "Put your board to work"
6. Tagging: "Spot a board? Tag it and earn"
7. Print job: "Print it, post it, get paid"
8. Reputation: "Work with people you can trust"

`description.txt` holds the app name, short description, subtitle, promotional text, keywords and full description.
